# Icon sources

`mezzanine.svg` is a custom three-tone greyscale adaptation of the overlapping spotlights
and stage in Mezzanine Theater Diary's app icon. It is not an official brand asset.
Reference: https://www.theaterdiary.com/press/ (app-icon.png).
The press kit permits use of its images. Three grey tones distinguish the two beams and their lighter central overlap.

The SVG uses three explicit grey fills to retain the overlapping beams.
Match its displayed dimensions and alignment to the other social links. Put the accessible
name on the enclosing profile link and hide its SVG from assistive technology
when the link already supplies the name.

All other SVGs are from Font Awesome Free 7.x:
https://github.com/FortAwesome/Font-Awesome/tree/7.x/svgs
See FONT-AWESOME-LICENSE.txt (SVG icons: CC BY 4.0). Original notices are
retained in each SVG. They are vendored locally, with no runtime font or CDN.
